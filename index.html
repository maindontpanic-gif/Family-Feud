✦ GAME RULES & MECHANICS

A Group Formation

Participants go through several rounds of grouping challenges. Follow the number of members instructed by the host.

  1. Group yourselves into 3015 participants eliminated
  2. Group yourselves into 1713 participants remain
  3. Group yourselves into 510 participants remain
  4. Group yourselves into 48 participants remain
  5. Group yourselves into 36 participants remain

The final 6 participants advance to the Family Feud game.

B Family Feud Mechanics

Teams

2 competing teams, 3 members each. Each team gets 1 minute to:

  • Choose a team leader
  • Create a team name
  • Prepare a team chant

Game Format

3 rounds in total:

Round 1 · SingleRound 2 · SingleRound 3 · Double

★ Rules and Mechanics

1 Starting the Round

  • Team leaders are the first representatives of their teams.
  • The host presents a question.
  • The representative with the higher-value answer wins the face-off.
  • The winner chooses PLAY or PASS.

2 Play or Pass

  • PLAY: your team is responsible for answering the question.
  • PASS: the opposing team takes over and attempts to answer.

3 Answering Rules

  • No coaching or giving answers to teammates unless the host announces a discussion period.
  • If the playing team fails to complete the board and gets three strikes, the opposing team may steal.
  • During a steal, only the opposing team’s leader’s answer is accepted.

4 Next Rounds

  • The same format and mechanics apply to succeeding rounds.
  • Points earned in each round accumulate.
  • The third round has double points.
